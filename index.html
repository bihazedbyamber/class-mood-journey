03 What is this?

A quick mood check for our class

After a lesson, everyone can say how it felt, in a few seconds. All the notes together draw the mood of the class over time: the good days, the rough days, and what made the difference. That helps all of us see what works, made by the class, for the class.

How it works

  1. Pick a face, or a small + between two faces when you feel a bit of both.
  2. Say what happened in a few words (or tap a quick idea).
  3. Your sticky note lands on the Class moods page, newest first.

Anonymous by default

No login, no email. Your name is only added if you switch Posting as to My name. The choice is saved on your device only.

Kind by design

Swear words in English, Indonesian and Javanese get covered with a *censored* sticker (you can switch it off in ⚙ Settings). The admin can hide notes, check new notes first, or pause check-ins.

Make it yours

Three styles: Modern 2026, Scribblish and Modern Bold, each in light or dark mode. Find them under ⚙ Settings.

For the student admins

A few classmates act as moderators: from the community, for the community. Their 🔒 Admin desk sorts notes into piles, pins, hides or deletes them and shows the mood numbers, including the in-between moods like Cheerful or Stressed.
